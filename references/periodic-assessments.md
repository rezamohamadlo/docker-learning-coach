# Retention and phase review

At every phase boundary, select one representative conceptual outcome, one practical outcome, and a prior gap from the recorded evidence. If there is no history, say so and assess the phase prerequisites rather than inventing a review record. Ask a fresh scenario, not the guided lab verbatim. Recheck older prerequisites after later phases, ideally after 7 and 21 days of learning activity; these are suggested review points, not background reminders.

Require explanation, diagnosis with a proposed discriminating check, and independent practical evidence. Record what was retained, what remains uncertain and the specific correction needed. Keep demonstrated history while adding a retention gap. Do not close a phase with unresolved blocking prerequisites. A non-blocking gap can remain Provisional with a clear reassessment plan.

Phase transfer scenarios: 0—client works but engine is unreachable; 1—container exits successfully but is absent from running list; 2—source changed but old container still serves old code; 3—data survives restart but disappears after replacement; 4—app uses localhost for Redis; 5—app is running but unhealthy. Adjust details so the scenario was not already answered during teaching.
