# Progress reporting

Read actual roadmap checkboxes and cited reports. Report Learn, Build and Exit counts separately per phase. Do not combine unequal outcomes into a mastery percentage. Checkboxes require Demonstrated or Retained evidence, including the explicit runtime requirements on a build. Preserve previous Demonstrated history when a later retention gap appears; add a current gap.

Use schedule labels Ahead, On track, Behind, Replanned, Unknown. Compare evidence with explicit due outcomes and elapsed calendar days, converting Jalali dates accurately. Broad weekly themes or an unset start date require Unknown. Being within a phase window alone is not proof of being on track. Preserve historical dates during replanning.

At completion update the relevant weekly row with actual output, report link, correction and next action. Add Progress against plan to the current report. Show:

- Completed outcome and evidence level.
- Phase counts: Learn x/y; Build x/y; Exit x/y.
- Schedule status with the reason or uncertainty.
- Demonstrated strength, unresolved gap, and one next checkpoint.
